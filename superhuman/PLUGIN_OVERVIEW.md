Superhuman-style speed for BB:

- **Shortcuts.** Shortcut hints on a held modifier; **/** searches, **H**
  snoozes, **E** archives, and numbers open a new thread's menus, in any
  keyboard layout.
- **Universal Search.** Find commands, threads, and snoozed threads using both
  the typed query and its English/Russian keyboard equivalent.
- **Snooze.** Hide a thread until a time you pick or until its agent needs you,
  from the moon in its header or any thread menu; **Show snoozed threads** lists
  them.
- **Archive Button.** Archive a thread from its header, next to Snooze.
- **Search Actions.** In thread search, **⌘↵** unsnoozes a snoozed thread or
  unarchives an archived one instead of opening it in split.
- **Dismiss Cards.** A close button on the cards above a thread's message box
  for background work, child threads, and the pull request; a card stays
  hidden in its thread until its words change.
- **Mobile Layout.** Bottom bars, edge swipes, and haptics on phones, with a
  fixed interface scale and pinch-to-zoom images. Thread titles stay hidden.
- **Hide Title Bar.** In the macOS desktop app, no top row with back, forward,
  and the sidebar button; their shortcuts still work.
- **UI Polish.** Icons in one tone and a thread bar without the external editor
  picker, with fixes for Safari's layout, for a Tesla's keyboard, and for inline
  visualizations in BB's iOS app.
- **Brief Replies.** Agents answer in few words of plain English, after Andrej
  Karpathy's advice, in threads started once it is on.

Shortcuts work on desktop, outside text fields, by key position, so **H** is
**H** in a Russian layout too. Snooze sits before Archive in every thread menu.
To snooze from a sidebar row in one click, add it in the row menu's
**Customize row actions**. Universal Search and Search Actions are verified for
BB 0.47.0 and 0.47.1 and turn themselves off on other versions. Settings have a
switch for each feature, all on by default except Hide Title Bar and Brief
Replies; turning Snooze off brings snoozed threads back.

## More San Francisco Plugins

Superhuman is one of the San Francisco Plugins, a family of BB plugins. The
others:

- **Bookmarks**: save any chat message and jump back to it from the thread's
  right panel.
- **To-do**: a Notes-style to-do list on the New thread screen and in the
  right panel; snooze a to-do or start a thread from it.
- **Canvas**: images, text, and drawing in each thread, with screenshots your
  agent can read.
- **Annotate**: notes on the elements of a page in BB's Browser, sent to your
  agent with a screenshot.
- **Auto Update**: updates for the plugins you pick, installed every hour, day,
  or week.
- **Storage Cleaner**: what fills your Mac's disk, and cleanup of archived
  threads, worktrees, and caches.
- **Resource Monitor**: live CPU, RAM, and GPU bars for the BB server in the
  sidebar, with the numbers on hover.
- **San Francisco**: a quiet macOS-style theme after the Aside browser, with
  your pick of the eight macOS accent colors.
- **Pool Usage**: how much of each provider's capacity is in use and when it
  frees up, in the sidebar footer.

See all of them, with install steps, at
[github.com/rebryk/bb-plugins](https://github.com/rebryk/bb-plugins).
