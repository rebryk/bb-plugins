Make BB look like a calm, native Mac app, in the quiet style of the Aside
browser.

## A quiet window

The content sits on the gray window frame as one white card with soft corners.
Text uses the system font at 14 px, SF Pro on a Mac. Hierarchy comes from
shades of gray rather than lines: primary text is near black, and secondary
text a softer gray. Icons use a thinner stroke. Only the composer and what
floats above the page, such as menus, popovers, dialogs, toasts and the
command palette, cast a soft shadow. Dark mode follows the same rules, with a
near-black frame, a slightly lighter card and near-white text.

Text is at least as easy to read as in BB's default palette. Dark mode's card
is lighter than BB's, so its gray text, accent text and BB's terminal, diff,
status and code colors are brighter to match.

## Familiar places

In the sidebar, rows are a soft black and the open thread is a white pill.
When Search threads is visible in the navigation, it becomes a search field.
On a desktop the sidebar loses its row of back and forward buttons, and its
toggle moves into the thread's header, before the title. Thread and panel
headers lose their dividers. The composer gets larger corners, a soft shadow
and one round send button with an up arrow; Stop is the same circle with a
square. Above the composer, background work becomes one outlined card, and
queued messages a sheet tucked behind the composer. Right-panel tabs become
quiet pills, and an empty new tab centers its hint above outlined buttons.

## Settings like a document

Settings pages show a Settings › Page breadcrumb and a large page title.
Section headings are small caps, and rows sit in cards split by full-width
lines. Selects shrink to their text and use a double chevron. Buttons, inputs
and badges are lighter, and menus and popovers are compact.

## One accent color

Pick one of the eight macOS accent colors: Blue, Purple, Pink, Red, Orange,
Yellow, Green or Graphite. It underlines links and colors file names, focus
rings, text selection, switches and checkboxes; everything else stays
neutral. Provider marks keep their shapes but take the surrounding text color.

## Turn it on

Choose San Francisco under Settings → Appearance → Palette, then click an
accent swatch under Settings → San Francisco. BB applies the palette before
the first paint and a non-Blue accent a moment later, once plugins load. Light
and dark follow BB's Theme setting. Choose Default to return to BB's look. No
external account or service is required.

## More San Francisco Plugins

San Francisco is one of the San Francisco Plugins, a family of BB plugins. The
others:

- **Superhuman**: Superhuman-style speed for BB, with shortcut hints, snooze,
  a compact grid of navigation icons, and a phone layout.
- **Bookmarks**: save any chat message and jump back to it from the thread's
  right panel.
- **Pool Usage**: how much of each provider's capacity is in use and when it
  frees up, in the sidebar footer.

See all of them, with install steps, at
[github.com/rebryk/bb-plugins](https://github.com/rebryk/bb-plugins).
