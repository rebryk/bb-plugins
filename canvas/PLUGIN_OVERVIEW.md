Collect screenshots, write notes, and sketch feedback in each thread's Canvas.

## Images, notes, and drawing

Open **Canvas** in the thread's right panel. Paste a screenshot with **⌘V** /
**Ctrl+V**, drop an image, or choose **Add image**. Select, move, and resize
images; add text or yellow sticky notes; draw freehand or drag out an arrow.
Text grows as you type, with new lines only on **Enter**. Square sticky notes
wrap and fit their text automatically; resizing a note scales the text with it.
Double-click text or a note to edit within its selection frame. Hold **Shift**
for horizontal or vertical lines and arrows. Clicks and tiny movements do not
leave stray dots.
Colors and three visual size presets sit in the toolbar for one-click
selection. Drag annotations with a mouse in any tool; hold **Alt** / **Option**
to draw or write over them. Images move and
resize with **Select**, leaving the other tools free to annotate them.
Double-click an image with **Select** to crop it. Drag the edges or move the
frame, then click outside to save and deselect. **Escape** cancels; expand the
crop edges to reveal the original image again.
Copy selected elements with **⌘C** / **Ctrl+C**, cut with **⌘X** / **Ctrl+X**,
and paste with **⌘V** / **Ctrl+V**, within the same canvas or into another
thread. Copies preserve styles and layout; Undo removes a pasted group in one
step. Zoom and fit keep navigation simple.

## Capture a browser block

Desktop BB's built-in browser has a **Canvas** toolbar button. Hover to
highlight a block and click to capture its visible portion directly into the
thread's canvas. **↑** selects its parent; **Escape** cancels. Embedded frames
are captured as whole blocks. In web BB, paste or upload a screenshot instead.

## Show your agent

Agents can read saved element positions and text with **canvas_read**, or see
the entire board with **canvas_screenshot**, even when the panel is closed.
You can export the same view as a PNG. Newly installed tools are available on
the next agent session start.

## Saved per thread

The board saves locally on the BB server as JSON with separate images. Each
thread keeps its own canvas across panel closures and restarts. Failed saves
show **Retry** and retain a recovery draft across reloads in the current browser
tab when storage is available. Drafts stay isolated between tabs.
Deleting a thread removes its canvas. The controls follow your BB theme,
including San Francisco's accent colors.

## More San Francisco Plugins

Canvas is one of the San Francisco Plugins, a family of BB plugins. The others:

- **Superhuman**: Superhuman-style speed for BB, with shortcut hints, snooze,
  a compact grid of navigation icons, and a phone layout.
- **Bookmarks**: save any chat message and jump back to it from the thread's
  right panel.
- **San Francisco**: a quiet macOS-style theme after the Aside browser, with
  your pick of the eight macOS accent colors.
- **Pool Usage**: how much of each provider's capacity is in use and when it
  frees up, in the sidebar footer.

See all of them, with install steps, at
[github.com/rebryk/bb-plugins](https://github.com/rebryk/bb-plugins).
