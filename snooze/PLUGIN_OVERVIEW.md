Hide a thread until you need it, the way Superhuman snoozes email.

## Snooze from the header or the palette

Click the moon in the thread header, or run **Snooze thread** from the command
palette, and pick when the thread comes back: **Later today**, **Tomorrow**,
**Next week**, or the time you used last. Or type your own, like `8 am`,
`in 2 hours`, `3 days`, `fri 3pm`, or `aug 7`, and the first row shows the
moment it means. The thread and its sub-threads leave the sidebar, BB opens the
next thread, and a toast offers **Undo**.

## Back when it matters

A snoozed thread returns at its time, marked unread. It returns at once when its
agent finishes a turn or fails, or when it or one of its sub-threads needs an
answer, so snoozing never hides a question. Snoozes that came due while BB was
off wake as soon as it starts.

## Every snoozed thread in one list

**Show snoozed threads** lists the snoozed threads with their wake times,
soonest first. Type to filter, press Enter to look at a thread without waking
it, or press **⌘↵** to unsnooze it. A snoozed thread's moon is filled, and its
picker offers **Unsnooze** first. Neither command has a default key, so bind
them in Settings → Keyboard. On phones, the picker and the list open in BB's
bottom drawer.

## On your BB server

Snoozes are stored on your BB server, so every device shows the same ones.
Nothing is sent to an agent or an external service. While the plugin is disabled
or removed, nothing wakes its snoozed threads, so unsnooze them before you
remove it.

## More San Francisco Plugins

Snooze is one of the San Francisco Plugins, a family of BB plugins. The others:

- **Super Hotkeys**: Superhuman-like keyboard navigation, with shortcut hints,
  slash search, and number keys that set up a new thread.
- **Bookmarks**: save any chat message and jump back to it from the thread's
  right panel.
- **Dia Sidebar**: the sidebar's navigation as a compact, wrapping grid of
  icons.
- **Pool Usage**: how much of each provider's capacity is in use and when it
  frees up, in the sidebar footer.
- **Server Switcher**: if you run several servers on bb connect, one click opens
  the next online one.

See all of them, with install steps, at
[github.com/rebryk/bb-plugins](https://github.com/rebryk/bb-plugins).
