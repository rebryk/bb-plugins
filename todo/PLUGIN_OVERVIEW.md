Keep your next to-dos one glance from a new thread, and start a thread from
any of them.

## A list under the composer

The New thread screen shows your to-dos right under the composer, like a list
in Notes: each one a round checkbox and a line of text. **Enter** adds the next
to-do, **Backspace** on an empty one removes it, and the handle drags a to-do
into place. Check a to-do and it moves to **Done**, a list that expands below;
uncheck it there to bring it back, or **Clear** the list.

## Snooze

Snooze a to-do until later today, tomorrow, the weekend, next week, or a date
and time you pick. It hides under **Snoozed** and comes back at the top of the
list, highlighted, when its time comes.

## Start a thread

A to-do's arrow button puts its text into the New thread composer.

## In the right panel

Choose **To-do** from the right panel's **+** menu to keep the list beside an
open thread. There is one list for the whole BB server, so desktop and phone
show the same to-dos.

## More San Francisco Plugins

To-do is one of the San Francisco Plugins, a family of BB plugins. The others:

- **Superhuman**: Superhuman-style speed for BB, with shortcuts and search in
  any keyboard layout, snoozed threads, and a phone layout.
- **Bookmarks**: save any chat message and jump back to it from the thread's
  right panel.
- **Canvas**: images, text, and drawing in each thread, with screenshots your
  agent can read.
- **Annotate**: notes on the elements of a page in BB's Browser, sent to your
  agent with a screenshot.
- **Auto Update**: updates for the plugins you pick, installed every hour, day,
  or week.
- **Storage Cleaner**: what fills your Mac's disk, and cleanup of archived
  threads, worktrees, and caches.
- **Resource Monitor**: live CPU, RAM, and GPU bars for the BB server in the
  sidebar, with the numbers on hover.
- **San Francisco**: a quiet macOS-style theme after the Aside browser, with
  your pick of the eight macOS accent colors.
- **Pool Usage**: how much of each provider's capacity is in use and when it
  frees up, in the sidebar footer.

See all of them, with install steps, at
[github.com/rebryk/bb-plugins](https://github.com/rebryk/bb-plugins).
